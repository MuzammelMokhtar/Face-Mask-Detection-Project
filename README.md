# PROBLEM STATEMENT
Controlling the disease's spread becomes of utmost importance in the current scenario
when the entire world is watching. The governing bodies have been recommending
numerous norms and guidelines for the same. The most effective preventative
measure among the different recommendations is to wear a mask on the face to lessen
the spread of COVID-19. 

# OBJECTIVES
• To ensure that the greatest number of individuals heed the warnings made by
respected health organization
• To automatically monitor the large population of human, whether they wear
facemasks
• To implement artificial intelligence for facemask detection with above 90%
Accuracy


# METHODOLOGY

Dataset was acquired from the open-source GitHub repository that have two classes
which are with mask (1,915 pictures) and without mask (1,918 pictures). Figures 1 &
2 show the dataset for with and without mask, respectively. The dataset, code, and
instruction can be found in this website (Face-Mask-Detection/dataset at master ·
balajisrinivas/Face-Mask-Detection (github.com)
